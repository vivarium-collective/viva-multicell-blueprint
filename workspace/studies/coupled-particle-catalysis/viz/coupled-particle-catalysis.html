Coupled cell — Smoldyn particle population catalyses an ODE (mode B)

A Smoldyn particle sim (A+B→C, confined to the cell polygon) catalyses an ODE S→P: the live particle C count sets the ODE rate constant each step. As the particle population builds, the coupled product P accelerates (concave-up), while a fixed-rate control (the same ODE driven by the run-mean count, 41) stays linear — the shape difference is the coupling.
