Cross-paradigm equivalence: fate yes, exact position no, and the test has power

Q Under replication, are the three mechanical substrates EQUIVALENT (not merely "not-distinguishable") on cell fate and on exact spatial position when one backend-neutral blueprint is run through `blueprint_pipeline`, and does the test correctly REJECT when mechanics (not the shared mechanism) drives the outcome?

Report
Notebook
Figures

Claim

Run through `blueprint_pipeline` on CPM, vertex and center-based (viva-munk), the ONE Sego 2022 blueprint gives backend-neutral cell FATE and backend-specific exact POSITION, and the equivalence test is powerful enough to tell them apart. FATE agreement is statistically EQUIVALENT for every backend pair within a pre-declared +/-0.05 margin (run-cluster 90% CI [-0.0034, +0.0008] for cpm-vertex). Exact-position agreement is NOT equivalent for any pair at +/-0.10, and it orders by how different the geometries are: cpm-munk +0.093 < cpm-vertex +0.126 < vertex-munk +0.252 (the cpm-munk point estimate sits below the 0.10 margin but its CI [0.034, 0.159] does not fit inside +/-0.10, so equivalence still cannot be claimed). A mechanics-dominated positive-control arm REJECTS equivalence for every pair with the same test (cpm-vertex Delta +0.325), so a "pass" on fate is informative. Judged against the same-backend seed null, the N=400 capstone fate metric fails to reject a substrate difference, while infected-patch occupancy IoU and patch location now sit outside both same-backend baselines for the current vertex substrate: a candidate substrate signal, not causally attributed.

Background

Question Under replication, are CPM, vertex and center-based EQUIVALENT (not merely "not-distinguishable") when one blueprint is run through `blueprint_pipeline` -- on cell fate, on exact spatial position, or both -- and does the test correctly reject when mechanics dominates?

Model & approach

Three arms, one machinery. (A) EQUIVALENCE GRID: run the full Sego blueprint on ALL THREE paradigms over rules-seeds {0..5} (N=120). For EACH backend pair (cpm-vertex, cpm-munk, vertex-munk) compute, per metric, the same-backend agreement (within-backend seed pairs of both members) and the cross-backend agreement (all 36 seed pairs); apply metrics.equivalence_tost (Welch TOST) + a run-cluster bootstrap against a pre-declared margin: +/-0.05 for fate agreement, +/-0.10 for the strict exact-position spatial agreement (centroids within one cell diameter, 5 um); split by cell class (epithelium / immune). (B) NULL-CONTROL CAPSTONE: compile the Sego blueprint onto CPM and vertex at a reduced scale (50x50-site patch, 6x6 epithelial block + 12-cell latent immune pool); for 3 CPM x 3 vertex rules-seeds at N=400 (and a lighter N=150 CI behavior test) compute per-cell fate agreement, infected-patch occupancy IoU and centroid distance, final epithelial area ratio and the vertex I-count's fraction inside the CPM >=3-seed stochastic envelope, each RELATIVE to the same-backend baselines (scripts/regen_capstone_metrics.py); render spatial_playback, dynamics_overlay and divergence maps (the center-based paradigm is a third panel / series). (C) POSITIVE CONTROL: run a mechanics-only blueprint (workspace/blueprints/mechanics_divergence.yaml: types A/B, A self-adhesive and repelled by B, a chemo field secreted by B with strong chemotaxis on A) on all three paradigms over rules-seeds {0,1,2} (N=80) and apply the SAME equivalence_tost (margin +/-0.10). Figures and results JSON are regenerated by scripts/regen_equivalence.py and scripts/render_study_figures.py.

Key assumptions

  • Equivalence margin is pre-declared, not fitted: +/-0.05 absolute fate agreement, +/-0.10 spatial agreement; neither is yet biologically justified.
  • The same-backend seed-to-seed spread (with mechanics seeded per replicate, mechanics_seed = rules_seed) is the matched null; equivalence and every cross-backend metric are judged against it, never against zero.
  • Spatial agreement is a STRICT exact-position metric (cohort centroids within 5 um = one cell diameter) at the final frame; it is a representation-sensitive readout by design.
  • Reduced scale (50x50 patch, 6x6 epithelial block, N=120 grid / N=400 capstone), faithful Sego rates (no tuning); 1 grid unit = 1 um.
  • Seed provenance: the N=400 table builds same-backend baselines from CPM seeds {0,1,2} AND vertex seeds {0,1,2} and the cross term from all 9 pairs; the lighter N=150 behavior test pins the relationship with CPM {0,1,2} vs vertex seed 0. Vertex is near-degenerate across rules-seeds at this scale (vertex-vs-vertex sd 0.006 vs CPM 0.025).
  • Immune recruitment is activation of a PRE-PLACED LATENT pool at the tissue margin; the systemic-ODE inputs V/L/C/F have no producer yet and sit at 0, so the immune arm is barely exercised at this scale.
  • The center-based model is deliberately NOT calibrated to the other two (docs/mechanics.md), so it is a genuinely independent representation.
  • The positive-control regime is mechanics-only (no rules/ODE/immune) with deliberately strong adhesion and chemotaxis knobs; nothing is tuned to hit a target, and the same metric/TOST/margin as the equivalence grid are used.

Limitations

What this study explicitly does NOT validate (prevents overclaim).

  • Biological fidelity to Sego 2022 is NOT validated: a CPM self-envelope is an internal consistency check, not a match to digitized Sego figures or measured flu-lung data (BP-08).
  • 'Runs the model' carries NAMED DEVIATIONS: V/L/C/F have no producer (sit at 0) and immune recruitment is a latent-pool approximation (BP-05); the immune arm is barely exercised at this scale.
  • Reduced scale and modest steps (50x50, N=120 / N=400, 3-6 seeds): a demonstration, not paper scale; the epidemic is a slow onset at faithful rates, so any substrate signal is small relative to seed noise. Fate equivalence at larger/longer scale is a follow-up.
  • The +/-0.05 fate and +/-0.10 spatial margins are pre-declared, not biologically justified (BP-06); a fail-to-reject on the capstone metrics is a null, not equality.
  • Exact-position agreement is NOT equivalent for any pair, and the vertex epithelium wanders ~5x less than the CPM's (motility capped so the mesh never folds; see substrate-behavior-at-scale), which narrows the vertex-vs-vertex positional spread and contributes to the gap.
  • The capstone IoU and patch-location metrics now separate the backends for the current vertex substrate (IoU 0.053 vs 0.100/0.299; 5.7 vs 3.3 um); this is a candidate substrate signal not yet causally attributed, and the dynamics band is the narrow CPM 3-seed envelope, not a Sego figure.
  • The positive control is an artificial mechanics-only regime with a strong gradient + differential adhesion: it shows the test has POWER but not sensitivity to realistic influenza-relevant discrepancies; the cause of its divergence (sorting / positional dynamics) is not isolated and its fate is static. Power should be mapped by graded injected deviations (BP-06, BP-07).
  • Equivalence is on fate and exact centroid position; a morphology-level equivalence (shape / pattern) is not claimed. Immune contact differs by design (same-layer edge on CPM vs margin-attached polygon on vertex).
  • Parameters are the faithful Sego 2022 values (no tuning of the biology); the vertex MECHANICS knobs are calibrated to CPM behaviour, never to make fates agree.
